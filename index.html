About this project

Created by Lara Washington

A generous interface for exploring species occurrence data in New York City via iNaturalist’s API, inspired by ALA Lens and Designing for biodiversity data in place.

Most Observed

Species with the most research-grade observations inside the circle.

Most Distinctive

Locally distinctive species, scored by comparing each species’ observations inside the circle with its observations state-wide (New York or New Jersey):

(species countlocal total) ÷ (state-wide species countstate total)

Only species with at least 10 observations inside the circle are scored.

Labels

Native, introduced and at-risk labels based on iNaturalist's listings by state.
